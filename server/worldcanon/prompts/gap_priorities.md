# Gap Priorities

The categories below are checked in order — once the entity has facts on file
that cover the first gap, move on to the next. Comments are ignored by the
parser; only `## <type>` sections and their numbered lists are read.

## character

1. Motivation / what they want
2. Defining wound or origin
3. Key relationships
4. Internal contradiction
5. Fears
6. Secrets
7. Daily habits
8. Skills and weaknesses
9. Beliefs and values

## place

1. History
2. Geography
3. Who lives there
4. What makes it distinct
5. What it's like to be there
6. Economic role
7. Conflicts
8. Secrets

## faction

1. Founding
2. Goals
3. Opposition
4. Organization
5. Rituals
6. Recruitment
7. Splits and factions-within
8. Secrets

## item

1. Origin
2. What it does
3. Costs / limits
4. Who has used it
5. Where it is now
6. Who wants it

## event

1. When
2. Who
3. Cause
4. What changed
5. Who remembers
6. What's debated about it

## system

1. Core mechanism
2. Costs and limits
3. Who can use it
4. How it's learned
5. Historical origin
6. Forbidden uses
7. Exceptions and edge cases
