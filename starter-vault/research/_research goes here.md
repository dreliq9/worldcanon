# Research

Notes about real-world stuff that informs your world. Examples:

- Articles about pre-industrial fishing economies
- Photos of Cornish coastlines (link, not the image)
- A summary of a book you read about Breton folk magic
- Your own typed transcripts of interviews, podcasts, documentaries

The plugin indexes this folder so `Canon: Ask about my world` and
`Canon: Search` can surface research notes alongside your fiction.

Keep it loose. Research is for reference, not for canon. Don't worry
about formatting it like an entity sheet.

You can delete this note once you have real research.
