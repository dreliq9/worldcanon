# Stillweaving

The coastal magic of holding things in place. A Stillweaver can pin a
wave mid-crest, freeze a falling object, suspend a person's heartbeat
for a long enough breath to save their life — or end it.

It is a quiet discipline. Practitioners are rare. The lighthouses on
the coastal headlands suppress the practice within their light — no one
has ever explained why.

## Rules

- rule: "Stillweaving cannot be performed during a thunderstorm"
  status: canon

- rule: "Lighthouses on the coastal headlands suppress Stillweaving within their lit arc"
  status: canon

- rule: "Holding a person's heartbeat still for more than seven seconds is fatal to both the Stillweaver and the subject"
  status: canon

- rule: "Stillweaving fades from a practitioner after about thirty years of disuse"
  status: draft
