# Brackwater

A small port town at the mouth of the Bracken River. Once a fishing
village, now a quiet stop on the coastal trade route. The headland to the
north holds a lighthouse rebuilt within the last decade — the original
was destroyed in the storm of [[Lyra Vance]]'s childhood.
