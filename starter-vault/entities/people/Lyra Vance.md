# Lyra Vance

Lyra is the protagonist of the first arc. Raised in [[Brackwater]] by her
grandmother after her mother vanished at sea. She is one of the last
practitioners of [[Stillweaving]] on the coastal headlands.

She returned to Brackwater after twenty years away, ostensibly to settle
her grandmother's estate — but the rebuilt lighthouse, and the keeper
inside it, have other plans for her.
